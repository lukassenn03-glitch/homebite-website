How it works

Three steps to a box from home

Step 1

Choose the occasion

Birthday, exam season, a cold that won't quit. Each occasion gets its own box design and card.

Step 2

Fill it from one country

Pick a country and choose up to 10 products. The typical ones for your occasion come first. Every product is sourced from producers in that country.

Step 3

Add your note and send

Write a personal note we print on a card, then ship it to yourself or straight to them.
